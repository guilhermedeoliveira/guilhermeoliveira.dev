PostgreSQL performance work is easiest when you keep the feedback loop close:
measure the query, inspect the plan, change one thing, and measure again.

Indexes are powerful, but they are not decoration. The best improvements often
come from understanding cardinality, removing stale assumptions, and noticing
when maintenance work has fallen behind the write pattern.
