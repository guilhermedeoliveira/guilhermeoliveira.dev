---
title: "Why I write small tools"
summary: "Notes on building simple, composable tools that age well."
date: 2024-03-08
tags: ["engineering", "tools"]
category: "Engineering"
---

Small tools are easier to finish, easier to delete, and easier to trust. They
make a sharp promise instead of trying to become a platform before anyone has
used them twice.

The best ones fit into existing workflows without ceremony. They solve one
annoying edge, expose a simple interface, and leave room for the next tool to
do its part.
