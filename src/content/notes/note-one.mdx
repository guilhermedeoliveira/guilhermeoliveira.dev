---
title: "Designing APIs that last"
summary: "Practical patterns for versioning, evolvability and keeping clients happy."
date: 2024-05-12
tags: ["apis", "versioning"]
category: "APIs"
---

Durable APIs leave room for change without making clients feel like the ground
is moving underneath them. The trick is to make compatibility a product
constraint, not a late-stage documentation chore.

Good API design starts with explicit contracts, predictable error surfaces, and
clear deprecation windows. Versioning helps, but the deeper work is shaping
responses so new capabilities can arrive without invalidating old assumptions.
