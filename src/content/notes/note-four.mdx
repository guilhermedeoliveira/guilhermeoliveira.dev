---
title: "Building offline-first apps that feel native"
summary: "Patterns for reliability without compromising UX."
date: 2024-03-22
tags: ["frontend", "offline-first"]
category: "Frontend"
---

Offline-first applications feel good when the local path is treated as the
primary experience. The interface should respond immediately, then reconcile
with the network without making the user supervise the machinery.

That means investing in conflict states, queue visibility, and copy that tells
the truth without sounding alarming. Reliability is not just storage; it is how
the whole product behaves under uncertainty.
