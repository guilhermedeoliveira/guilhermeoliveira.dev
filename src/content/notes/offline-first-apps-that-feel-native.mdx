Offline-first is not a loading strategy. It is a promise that the user's work remains theirs even when the network disappears.

## Local truth

The application should be useful before it phones home. That means local reads, local writes, and a sync layer that can explain conflicts without panic.

## Visible sync

People tolerate eventual consistency when the interface tells the truth. Show what is saved locally, what is syncing, and what needs attention.

## Native feeling

The app feels native when latency is not part of every decision. The interface should move at the speed of thought, then reconcile calmly.
