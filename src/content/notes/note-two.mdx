---
title: "Idempotency keys are underrated"
summary: "Make retries safe and your life easier."
date: 2024-04-28
tags: ["backend", "retries"]
category: "Backend"
---

Retries are inevitable, so systems should treat duplicate requests as a normal
case. Idempotency keys give clients a small, explicit tool for making that
normal case safe.

The pattern is especially useful around payments, provisioning, queue
handoffs, and every workflow where a timeout does not prove that nothing
happened. Store the key, store the outcome, and make the second request boring.
