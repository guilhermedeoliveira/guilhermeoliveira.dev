PostgreSQL performance work starts with observation. Guessing at indexes is a fast way to trade one invisible problem for another.

## Start with the plan

Use `EXPLAIN (ANALYZE, BUFFERS)` when you can. The query planner tells a story about row estimates, access paths, and where time actually went.

## Bloat is a product problem

Vacuum settings are operational details, but bloat often points to product behavior: churn-heavy tables, soft deletes, or data models that update too much too often.

## Indexes have rent

Every index makes reads easier and writes heavier. The right question is not whether an index helps one query, but whether it earns its maintenance cost.
