Markdown is excellent until the surrounding system needs guarantees.

Typed content collections are a practical middle ground. The writing stays portable, but metadata becomes checkable. Dates are dates. Tags are arrays. Drafts can be filtered out consistently.

## Why it matters

Once content is typed, it can safely drive pages, RSS feeds, search indexes, project lists, and internal tools.

That is the useful point: not structure for its own sake, but structure that lets the site grow without becoming fragile.
