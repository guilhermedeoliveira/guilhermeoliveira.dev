Software that acts on your behalf should be designed around review.

That does not mean every action needs a modal. It means the system should leave enough evidence that a human can understand what happened without reconstructing the whole run.

## Good traces

A good trace says what input was used, what rule fired, what output changed, and what confidence level the system has.

For personal operations, this can be as simple as a log line, a table row, and a pending action marker.

## Bad magic

Bad magic hides the important part. It says "done" without showing the path.
