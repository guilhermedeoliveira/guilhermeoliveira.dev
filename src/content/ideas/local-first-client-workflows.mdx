title: "Local-first client workflows"
summary: "What would client operations look like if the source of truth lived locally first and synced outward only when needed?"
date: 2026-05-04
tags: ["local-first", "workflow", "tools"]
status: "exploring"
---

The idea is a small toolkit for client work where local files are the primary system of record.

Invoices, notes, drafts, research, and delivery artifacts could all have local lifecycle states before anything is uploaded, shared, or archived.

The open question is how much structure is enough before the tool becomes heavier than the work.
