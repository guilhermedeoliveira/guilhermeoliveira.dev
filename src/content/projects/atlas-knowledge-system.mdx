Atlas is a local-first personal knowledge system. Raw source material lands in `raw/`, structured notes live in `wiki/`, and every operation appends to an auditable log.

The interesting part is not the wiki. The interesting part is the operating model around it: ingestion commands, financial parsers, medical document routing, calendar conventions, lint checks, and query workflows that keep the system honest over time.

## Design pressure

Most personal knowledge systems fail because capture is easy and maintenance is expensive. Atlas reverses that pressure by making maintenance explicit:

- Every source has a lifecycle.
- Every wiki page has frontmatter and backlinks.
- Every operation is logged.
- Stale claims and conflicts have visible markers.

The system is intentionally plain. Markdown, Make, and small scripts are easier to inspect than a clever app that becomes another thing to maintain.

## Engineering lesson

The durable part of a personal tool is not the interface. It is the contract between input, transformation, and review.
