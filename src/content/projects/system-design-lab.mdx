The system design lab is a structured learning area for architecture material. It separates curriculum modules, standalone concepts, Q&A sessions, external article notes, and critiques.

The structure matters because system design study can turn into a pile of disconnected facts. The lab gives every note a home and makes relationships easier to see.

## What it optimizes for

- Fast retrieval during review.
- Clear distinction between concepts and case studies.
- Space for critique, not just summaries.
- Gradual growth without premature subfolders.

## Engineering lesson

Information architecture is software architecture for thought. Naming conventions are APIs for your future self.
