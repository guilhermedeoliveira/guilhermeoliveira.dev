Relay is a small event delivery service built around boring reliability:
durable queues, explicit retry state, and enough observability to debug
delivery behavior under load.

## Design notes

The service favors straightforward operational controls over clever broker
magic. Every retry, handoff, and dead-letter path is visible to the teams that
depend on it.
