Termio keeps repeatable terminal layouts close at hand. It stores panes,
working directories, commands, and hotkeys so context can be restored without a
long setup ritual.

## Why it exists

The project grew from the small but persistent cost of rebuilding the same
workspace every morning. Termio makes that state explicit, portable, and easy
to share across machines.
