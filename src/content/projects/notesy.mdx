## Product angle

notesy explores a quieter kind of notes app: fast capture, linked references, and no pressure to build a public garden before the thought is ready.

## Technical angle

The local database is the primary interface. Sync is treated as a transport concern, not as the product's center of gravity.

## Open questions

The hardest part is not editing text. It is designing retrieval that helps without turning the app into a surveillance-flavored assistant.
