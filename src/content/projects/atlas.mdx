## Why it exists

atlas is a personal operating system for notes, source documents, routines, health logs, finance ledgers, and research. It favors a plain-file wiki because the knowledge should outlive any single app.

## How it works

Raw files land in an inbox. Scripts extract structured facts, then the wiki is updated with cross-links, logs, and action markers. The important constraint is provenance: every durable claim should point back to source material.

## Engineering taste

The project intentionally uses boring primitives. The payoff is that the system can be inspected, linted, backed up, and repaired with ordinary tools.
