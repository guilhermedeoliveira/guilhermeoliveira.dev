Atlas summarizes engineering flow without sending raw activity to a third
party. It keeps the useful parts of developer analytics while leaving the
individual trace on the developer machine.

## What it tracks

The product focuses on trends that help teams rebalance work: review queues,
handoff delays, and places where automation could remove repeated toil.
