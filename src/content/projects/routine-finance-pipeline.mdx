This pipeline handles the boring but important work around a small services company: invoices, notas fiscais, bank statements, taxes, Drive folders, and monthly checklists.

The workflow detects document type from filenames and PDF content, extracts amounts and dates, uploads files to the right Drive location, updates the monthly routine page, and leaves a log entry.

## What made it hard

The hard part was not parsing one document. It was keeping the process safe when documents arrive in slightly different forms:

- Pending and received invoices look similar.
- Fiscal documents belong to competence months, but taxes follow issue dates.
- Bank statements may confirm tasks for a previous month.

The pipeline works because it encodes those domain rules explicitly instead of hiding them in chat memory.

## Engineering lesson

Operational automation should be conservative. It should explain what it did, keep metadata visible, and make manual review cheap.
