This site is designed to feel less like a portfolio template and more like a public engineering notebook.

Projects sit in the center because they are the strongest proof of judgment. Writing stays visible in the right rail because essays and notes explain the thinking behind the work.

## Design choices

- Astro keeps the default payload small.
- MDX keeps articles flexible without requiring a CMS.
- Content collections make projects, articles, and ideas typed.
- Pagefind adds search without a server.

## Engineering lesson

The best personal site is not a resume page. It is a durable publishing system.
